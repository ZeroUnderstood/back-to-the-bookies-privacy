1. Overview

Back to the Bookies is a standalone historical football betting simulation and statistical replay game. We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. The application is designed to be fully functional without collecting, harvesting, or transmitting any personal information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collected: The application does not collect, record, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal, sensitive, or device-identifying data.
  • Local Storage Only: Game progress, season state, virtual bankrolls, and user preferences are stored on your device in local application storage (such as browser localStorage). We do not receive this data. If Android backup is enabled for your device, Android may include app data in your personal backup.
  • No Internet Permission (Android App): The Android application package is built with no network permissions (INTERNET permission is stripped). The application operates 100% offline and cannot transmit data across a network.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

  • No Advertising: The application contains no advertising frameworks or third-party ads.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: We do not include tracking SDKs, analytics suites, or telemetry tools.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your game data. Any device backup is managed by your Android backup provider and settings.

4. Virtual Currency & Simulated Gaming

Back to the Bookies uses virtual play bankroll (£5,000 start) for historical simulation purposes only. The application does not support or involve real-money transactions, deposits, withdrawals, or prizes.

5. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for a general sports simulation audience. Because the app collects no personal data whatsoever from any user, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any future revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
